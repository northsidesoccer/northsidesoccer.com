Thank you for your interest in Northside Soccer. Here is some information regarding our league:

Who is Northside Soccer?
: We are a small recreational soccer league run completely by volunteers. Our motto is “everybody plays” and we focus on ensuring all children and families have a good experience.

What is the difference between Rec, Rec Plus and Travel Soccer?
: <p>Recreational (Rec) Soccer is for beginners and children/families  are looking for a good, low-stress soccer environment. Rec teams only play other teams in our league. It is good for children who want to play soccer as well as other sports/activities.</p>

  Rec Plus Soccer is for children/families looking for slightly more competitive soccer but not wanting to make the commitment required for travel soccer. Rec Plus teams play other local area teams. There are no try-outs allowed.

  Travel Soccer is competitive soccer, often significantly more expensive, for children/families that are looking to make a significant soccer commitment.

  Northside Soccer offers only Rec and Rec Plus teams which are based on the following age groups:

  * Rec: U6 to U10
  * Rec Plus: U12 to U19

How are age groups determined?
: Age groups are determined based on the age of your child as of the 1st of August previous to the start of the season (e.g. August 1, 2022 for Fall 2022 and Spring 2023 seasons).
  * U6: 4 to 5 years of age
  * U8: 6 to 7 years of age
  * U10: 8 to 9 years of age
  * U12: 10 to 11 years of age
  * U14: 12 to 13 years of age
  * U16: 14 to 15 years of age
  * U19: 16 to 18 years of age

Where are practices and games held?
: All practices are held at [Sahm Park]. Reference the [rules](/rules) for game locations.

When are practices?
: <p>Rec teams practice on Tuesdays or Thursdays. Select your preference during registration. Most teams practice at 6:30pm for 45 minutes to an hour, at the discretion of the coaches.</p>

  Rec Plus teams practice both Tuesdays and Thursdays, typically for around 90 minutes.

When are games?
: Rec games are on Saturdays. We typically play seven Saturdays through the season, skipping Labor Day or Memorial Day weekends.
  * The U6 league plays at 9:00am.
  * The U8 league plays at 10:15am.
  * The U10 league plays at 9:00am.

  To fit in an eighth game, we typically play once on a Tuesday or Thursday in lieu of the regular Rec practice.

  Rec Plus games are typically Saturdays and Sundays. Scheduling is done in concert with other local clubs. Some weekends are off, and some weekends may see a game both days.

What are the registration fees?
: <p>The Rec registration fee is $115. The Rec Plus Fee is $130. There is a $10 early bird discount if you register before the deadline listed on the [calendar], and a $5 per  sibling discount is provided for families with more than one child participating.</p>

  We require a credit card payment during registration. We may charge a $15 fee in case of a refund. For any questions or issues, please contact our finance team at [treasurer@northsidesoccer.com](mailto:treasurer@northsidesoccer.com).

What is included in that fee?
: The registration fee covers jersey, socks, team picture, and free admission in a league-wide picnic (with bounce houses, face painting, etc.). In addition to these items, the fee for Rec teams include shorts, trophy, and two tickets for snacks at the concession stand after each game. Rec Plus players have an option to purchase shorts at time of registration.

When does the season start?
: Please see the [calendar][calendar].

How can I get involved?
: We are a volunteer-led organization. Please refer to Volunteer Duty in the [parent info](/parents) and either [contact us](/contact) or select what you would like to volunteer for during registration.
