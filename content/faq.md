Thank you for your interest in Northside Soccer. Here is some information regarding our league:

Who is Northside Soccer?
: We are a small recreational soccer league run completely by volunteers. Our motto is “everybody plays” and we focus on ensuring all children and families have a good experience.

What is the difference between Rec, Rec Plus and Travel Soccer?
: * Recreational (Rec) Soccer is for beginners and children/families  are looking for a good, low-stress soccer environment. Rec teams only play other teams in our league. It is good for children who want to play soccer as well as other sports/activities.

  * Rec Plus Soccer is for children/families looking for slightly more competitive soccer but not wanting to make the commitment required for travel soccer. Rec Plus teams play other local area teams. There are no try-outs allowed.

  * Travel Soccer is competitive soccer, often significantly more expensive, for children/families that are looking to make a significant soccer commitment.

  Northside Soccer offers only Rec and Rec Plus teams, which are based on the following age groups:

  * Rec: U6 to U10
  * Rec Plus: U12 to U19

How are age groups determined?
: See the [guidelines] for age ranges.

Where are practices and games held?
: All practices are held at [Sahm Park]. Reference the [guidelines] for game locations.

When are practices?
: * Rec teams practice one night per week.  Select your preference during registration.  Most teams practice at 6:30pm for 45 minutes to an hour, at the discretion of the coaches.
  * Rec Plus teams practice twice per week, typically for 90 minutes.  Practice nights are based on coaches' availability.  See the [guidelines] for practice nights.

When are games?
: * Rec games are mostly on Saturday morning.  We typically play seven Saturdays through the season, skipping Labor Day or Memorial Day weekends.  To fit in eight games, we may play midweek games.  These special weeks are noted on the [calendar].
  * Rec Plus games are typically Saturdays and Sundays.  Scheduling is done in concert with other local clubs.  Some weekends are off, and some weekends may see a game both days.

What are the registration fees?
: The Rec registration fee is $120. The Rec Plus Fee is $140. There is a $10 early bird discount if you register before the deadline listed on the [calendar], and a $10 per sibling discount is provided for families with more than one child participating.<p>

  We require a credit card payment during registration. We may charge a $15 fee in case of a refund. For any questions or issues, please contact our finance team at [treasurer@northsidesoccer.com](mailto:treasurer@northsidesoccer.com).

What is included in that fee?
: * The registration fee covers jersey, socks, team picture, and free admission to a league-wide picnic (with bounce houses, face painting, etc.) for all age levels.
  * The fee for Rec also includes shorts, a trophy, and two tickets for snacks at the concession stand after each game.
  * The fee for Rec Plus includes admission to a tournament during the season.

When does the season start?
: Please see the [calendar].

How can I get involved?
: We are a volunteer-led organization. Please refer to Volunteer Duty in the [conduct page][conduct] and either [contact us](/contact) or select what you would like to volunteer for during registration.
